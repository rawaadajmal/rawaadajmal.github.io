Privacy Policy — Tasbih Counter

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Rawaad Ajmal ("we", "our") operates the Tasbih Counter mobile app ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles when you use it.

2. Information We Collect

We do not ask for your name, email, or any account information. The App needs no account and no sign-up. Your dhikr counts, targets, daily count and streak are stored only on your device. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the App deletes the App's own stored data.

3. Permissions

The App uses only the VIBRATE permission, for a light vibration on each tap while counting. The App needs no internet connection and works fully offline.

4. Advertising and Analytics

The App shows no ads and uses no analytics. No advertising identifier is collected, and no usage data is sent to us or to any analytics service.

5. How We Use Information

Information stored on your device is used only to run the App (saving your counts, targets and streak). We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone. We operate no server for this App, and no data is sent to us.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will address it promptly.

7. Data Security

Your counts and settings are kept in your device's storage. No data is transmitted to our servers because we operate none for this App.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the Effective Date above and publish the new version at the same link.
