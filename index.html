Arrow Puzzle Master

A relaxing arrow puzzle game. Tap the arrows in the right order, clear the board, and travel through six themed worlds.

Get it on Google Play

About the game

Arrow Puzzle Master is a casual puzzle game built around one simple idea: every arrow on the board can only leave in one direction, and only when its path is clear. Find the right order, clear the board, and move forward — easy to learn, satisfying to master.

Features

  • Adventure mode: 300 levels across 6 themed zones — Forest, Desert, Ice, Volcano, Ocean and Space — each harder than the last, with a boss level at the end of every zone.
  • Daily Challenge: a new 7-day challenge journey with streaks. Clear a day to earn 1,000 points.
  • Points to Lives: turn your earned points into extra lives whenever you need them.
  • Six visual themes and gentle sound effects for a calm, focused game feel.
  • Plays offline — your progress is saved on your own device.
