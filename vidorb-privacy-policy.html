Privacy Policy — VidOrb

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Rawaad Ajmal ("we", "our") operates the VidOrb mobile app ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles when you use it.

2. Information We Collect

We do not ask for your name, email, or any account information. The App needs no account and no sign-up. Videos you download are saved only on your device, in your Downloads/VidOrb folder. Your download list and game scores are stored only on your device. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the App deletes the App's own stored data (downloaded video files in your Downloads folder stay until you delete them).

3. Links You Copy

When you tap the Orb, the App reads the link in your clipboard to find the video you want to download. The link is never shown on screen and is never sent to us — it is used only to fetch the video's information from the site the link came from (TikTok, Facebook, or Pinterest), and the video file is downloaded directly to your device. We operate no server of our own for this App, and no link or video information is sent to us.

4. Advertising and Analytics

The App shows no ads and uses no analytics. No advertising identifier is collected for ads, and no usage data is sent to us or to any analytics service.

5. How We Use Information

Information stored on your device is used only to run the App (saving your downloads and settings). We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will address it promptly.

7. Data Security

Downloaded videos and App data are kept in your device's storage. No data is transmitted to our servers because we operate none for this App.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the Effective Date above and publish the new version at the same link.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy: rawaadajmal.apps@gmail.com
